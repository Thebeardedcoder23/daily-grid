# Puzzle Spec — v1

## Format
- Grid: 11x11
- Difficulty target: NYT Monday
- Solve time target: about 10–15 minutes
- Platform: mobile-first web app
- Tone: playful, personal, lightly teasing; not overly sentimental
- Personal answers mixed with ordinary crossword fill so the puzzle still feels legitimate

Priority answers:
- MANDY — only acceptable form of Amanda
- HINGE — where the connection started

- ALCATRAZ — first date
- POPPY — the perfect cat
- KARL — the bad cat

## Important construction rule

COMMIT TO THE BIT is 14 letters without spaces and therefore cannot be one entry in an 11x11 grid. Keep the 11x11 format and represent the phrase through linked entries/clues (COMMIT and BIT), or through clue text, rather than forcing a rebus or enlarging the puzzle.

## Hidden reveal

The solved grid contains 14 ordinary cells whose solution letters, read in a predetermined sequence, spell:

WILL YOU MARRY ME

Those cells look ordinary during play. They are not circled or visually marked beforehand.

After every entry is correct:
1. Normal completion feedback.
2. Brief pause.
3. Selected cells highlight sequentially.
4. Letters are collected into the final message.
5. The screen yields visually to the real-world proposal; no long digital speech.

The app must never expose the hidden message before successful completion through visible UI, metadata, page title, or obvious labels.

## Clue voice examples

- HAYLEY: “The coolest girl in the world, according to Mandy (and no, it’s not her)”
- POPPY: “The perfect one, obviously”
- KARL: “The bad one, obviously”
- MANDY: “The only acceptable version of Amanda”
- COMMIT / BIT: callback to “commit to the bit” from Mandy’s Hinge profile

## Construction quality bar

- No two-letter entries.
- Prefer familiar Monday-level vocabulary.
- Avoid crosswordese when a cleaner fill exists.
- Personal entries should be fairly clued from Robert and Mandy’s shared context.
- Ordinary fill should be straightforward enough that Mandy does not get stuck.
- Do not force every personal memory into the grid.
